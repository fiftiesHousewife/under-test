# Expert time is booked as a budget of short question sessions

**Decided by:** Engagement Lead, with the Programme Sponsor · **Status:** proposed (agreed in discussion)

*This is a decision guide for adopting organisations. Each organisation accepts, narrows or replaces it in its own organisation repo.*

## Context and Problem Statement

Subject-matter experts are the scarcest resource. Asking them to review long drafts, or to answer questions whenever they come up, wastes their time and stalls work.

## Considered Options

* **Expert time is booked up front as a fixed number of short sessions (about 25 minutes) per area. Before each, the AI drafts rules with sources, compares them with current behaviour, and prepares a short list of questions; the expert answers; answers become specification rules. Unanswered questions stay recorded and block sign-off of intended behaviour, but not test writing**  
  Predictable demand on experts; a record of every answer
* **Ask experts as questions come up**  
  No planning; unpredictable and easily stalled
* **Experts review full written drafts**  
  Thorough but slow, and reading is the costly part

## Decision Outcome

Agreed in discussion; waiting for approval by pull request. Agreed in discussion: the first option, in the style of Example Mapping (rules, examples and open questions).

### Consequences

* Session budgets in the application testing plan.
* A session template, with the budget fields, in the application testing plan.
