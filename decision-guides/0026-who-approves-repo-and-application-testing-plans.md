# Who approves repo and application testing plans

**Decided by:** Steering Group · **Status:** proposed

*This is a decision guide for adopting organisations. Each organisation accepts, narrows or replaces it in its own organisation repo.*

## Context and Problem Statement

Plans are drafted with AI help. Someone accountable must approve each one.

## Considered Options

* **Repo plan approved by its owner; application plan approved by the application owner; both by pull request**  
  Accountability sits with owners; changes are visible
* **Central approval**  
  Bottleneck at this scale

## Decision Outcome

Proposed: the first option. Role names follow the engagement roles in `GLOSSARY.md`.

### Consequences

* The [plan template](../templates/testing-plan.md) and the [load-repo-work-into-the-issue-tracker](../.github/skills/load-repo-work-into-the-issue-tracker/SKILL.md) skill.
