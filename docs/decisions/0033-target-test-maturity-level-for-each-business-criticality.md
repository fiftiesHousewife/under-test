# Target test maturity level for each business criticality

**Decided by:** Test Quality Lead, approved by the Steering Group · **Status:** proposed

## Context and Problem Statement

Not every application needs the same depth of testing. Effort should follow importance.

## Considered Options

* **High: level 4 on key user journeys, plus level 5. Medium: level 3. Low: level 2**  
  A common starting point
* **Stricter**  
  Needs more subject-matter expert time, usually the real limit

## Decision Outcome

Proposed: these targets as a starting point.

### Consequences

* Targets in the assessment file; summaries show the share of applications at target.
