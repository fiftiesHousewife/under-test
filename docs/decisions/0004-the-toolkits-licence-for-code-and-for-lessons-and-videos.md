# The toolkits licence for code and for lessons and videos

**Decided by:** Programme Sponsor · **Status:** proposed (agreed in discussion)

## Context and Problem Statement

Without a licence, nobody may legally use, edit, rebrand or redistribute any part of the toolkit. Organisations' legal teams will check this first.

## Considered Options

* **Apache-2.0 for everything: code, tools, decision guides, lessons, and videos with their layers**  
  One licence; widely approved in companies; anyone may use, change, rebrand and redistribute, keeping the licence and notices and marking changed files; includes a patent grant
* **Apache-2.0 for code and tools; Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) for lessons, guides and videos**  
  Licence suited to each kind of content; two licences to explain
* **MIT for everything**  
  Shortest; no patent grant; less suited to prose and video
* **A single licence chosen by the sponsoring organisation**  
  Fits its policy; may not suit everyone else

## Decision Outcome

Agreed in discussion; waiting for approval by pull request. Agreed in discussion: Apache-2.0 for everything.

### Consequences

* `LICENSE` (Apache-2.0) and `NOTICE` at the repo root.
* Licence noted in each published artifact.
