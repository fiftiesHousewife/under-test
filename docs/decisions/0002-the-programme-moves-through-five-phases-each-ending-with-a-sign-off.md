# The programme moves through five phases, each ending with a sign-off

**Decided by:** Programme Sponsor · **Status:** proposed

## Context and Problem Statement

A rollout across thousands of repos needs clear points where we check results before going further.

## Considered Options

* **Five phases: set-up (ends when the Security and Platform Owner approves the environment and the roles are agreed); finding out what repos we have (ends when repos are classified and first batches proposed); pilots per technology stack and application type (ends when success criteria are met and testing guides written); rolling out in batches (each batch ends with its Application Owners' sign-off); handing over (ends when the client's teams run batches without the forward deployed team and the Toolkit Owner role has moved to them)**  
  Clear checkpoints; matches common practice
* **Continuous rollout with no phases**  
  Faster start; no agreed points to stop and check

## Decision Outcome

Proposed: the five phases. The Steering Group approves each move to the next phase.

### Consequences

* Backlog grouped by phase.
